## 1. Battle Formats

### Singles (3v3)

- **Team preview:** Bring 6 Pokémon, pick 3.
- **Field:** 1 Pokémon at a time.
- **Strategy:** Individual matchups, prediction, careful switching. Less emphasis on synergy.
- **Switching:** Safer, less punishable than Doubles.
- **Support moves:** Less common.
- **Battle duration:** Longer (frequent switching).
- **Ranked queue:** Separate from Doubles — its own ladder and season rewards.

### Doubles (4v4 bring, 2 at a time)

- **Team preview:** Bring 6 Pokémon, pick 4. First 2 picked are sent first.
- **Field:** 2 Pokémon at a time.
- **Strategy:** Synergy, weather, Trick Room, terrain, Tailwind, Helping Hand, Fake Out, Protect.
- **Switching:** Riskier (more variables).
- **Battle duration:** Shorter (high damage output).
- **Official VGC format:** All official tournaments use Doubles. Singles is a side mode.

### Timers (both formats)

| Rule              | Value                   |
| ----------------- | ----------------------- |
| Team preview      | 90 seconds              |
| Move time limit   | 45 seconds per turn     |
| Player total time | 7 minutes ("Your time") |
| Game time         | 20 minutes              |
| Auto-level        | All Pokémon → Level 50  |

## 2. Box Size Limit

### Default

- **30 Pokémon** for free-to-play players.

### Expansion Methods

| Method                      | Slots Added | Cost                  | Permanent?                          |
| --------------------------- | ----------- | --------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Rank up (Great Ball)        | +5          | Free (first time)     | Likely yes                          |
| Rank up (Ultra Ball)        | +5          | Free (first time)     | Likely yes                          |
| Rank up (Master Ball)       | +10         | Free (first time)     | Likely yes                          |
| Starter Pack                | +50         | One-time purchase     | Yes                                 |
| Membership (monthly/annual) | +1,000      | $4.99/mo or $49.99/yr | **No — drops when membership ends** |

### Critical Membership Risk

When membership expires, box capacity drops back to base limit. Pokémon beyond the cap become **inaccessible** until space is freed or membership renewed. **Do not let membership lapse without a plan.**

### "Returning" Pokémon (freeing box space)

- **Return to Ranch (Roster Ranch):** Permanent deletion. Cannot be recovered. No refund of VP/tickets.
- **Send back to Pokémon HOME:** Only for Pokémon originally from HOME. Safe — they retain all Champions training modifications when brought back later.
- **Release via Roster Ranch menu:** Go to Recruit menu → Return Pokémon → select Pokémon → confirm.

## 3. Daily Recruitment Mechanic (Roster Ranch)

### Free Daily Roll

- **Every 22 hours:** a lineup of 10 random Pokémon refreshes automatically.
- Players choose **only 1 Pokémon** from each lineup.
- Ranch Pokémon come with: 2 random stats maxed out, random nature, random ability, random moveset, random Poké Ball. Can have Marks. Can be Shiny.

### Speeding Up Refresh

- **Quick Coupons:** 1 coupon = 1 hour skipped. Need 22 for full refresh.
- **VP:** Can also spend VP to force a refresh.

### Trial (Rental) Recruitment

- **Free** — keeps the Pokémon for **7 days**.
- **Cannot be trained** (no stat/move/ability editing). Can only equip held items.
- Counts toward box space during the trial period.
- Can only do **1 Trial Recruitment per day**.

### Permanent Recruitment

- Costs **2,500 VP** or **1 Teammate Ticket**.
- Pokémon joins box permanently. Fully trainable.
- If unwanted, can be returned to Ranch (permanent deletion).

### Pokémon HOME Integration

- Pokémon transferred from HOME behave like permanently recruited Pokémon — fully trainable.
- Can be sent back to HOME freely.
- Retain Champions training modifications when returned to Champions later.
- Champions modifications do NOT propagate to other games.

### Affinity Tickets

- Consumable tickets used BEFORE refreshing the ranch lineup.
- **Type Affinity Tickets** — bias the next lineup toward a specific type (18 types available).
- **Ball Guaranteed Tickets** — force all Pokémon in next lineup to be inside a specific Poké Ball type (Ultra, Great, Poké, Net, Dive, Nest, Repeat, Timer, Luxury, Premier, Dusk, Heal, Quick, Rare).
- Earned from Achievements.

### Special Rosters

- Time-limited event rosters (e.g., Worlds Commemorative Roster, Global Challenge).
- Require special tickets to recruit from.

## 4. 3-Team Limit — Structure and Locking

### Default

- **F2P players get exactly 3 active Battle Team slots.**
- No way to increase this without paying.
- **Membership unlocks 15 additional slots** (total 18).

### Community Workaround: Replica Teams (Cloud Backup)

- The game's **Replica Teams** feature (meant for sharing) can be used as a personal archive.
- Players can generate a **Team ID** for any saved team (up to 10 Replica Team slots).
- To restore: enter the Team ID → overwrite an active slot → pay VP if Pokémon don't match the saved build.
- **VP cost is 0** if your current Pokémon exactly match the saved configuration.
- Effectively gives **13 free team slots** (3 active + 10 Replica archives).
- **Coordinate Teams** — a separate system that can hold up to 12 saved team configurations (archived blueprints, not instant-swap).

### What a Team ID Preserves

- 6 Pokémon species, movesets, held items, abilities, training choices.
- Does **NOT** preserve the Pokémon themselves — if you release a Pokémon, the team ID still exists but requires you to acquire/train that Pokémon again to use it.

## 5. Tiers, Bans, and Clauses

### Universal Clauses (all regulations)

| Clause             | Rule                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Species Clause** | No two Pokémon on a team may share the same National Pokédex number (including alternate forms like Rotom-Wash / Rotom-Heat). |
| **Item Clause**    | No two Pokémon on a team may hold the same item.                                                                              |

### Regulation M-A (Apr 8 – Jun 17, 2026)

- **Doubles (VGC format)** — Level 50 auto.
- **Banned categories:**
  - All Restricted / Box Legendaries
  - All Sub-Legendaries
  - All Mythical Pokémon
  - All Paradox Pokémon (past & future)
  - Treasures of Ruin (Chien-Pao, Chi-Yu, Ting-Lu, Wo-Chien)
  - Explicit: Koraidon, Miraidon
- **Banned ability:** Battle Bond (Greninja)
- **Mega Evolutions:** Legal (certain species only).

### Regulation M-B (Jun 17 – Sep 9, 2026)

- Added 22 Pokémon + 16 new Mega Evolutions.
- Same ban framework; broader legal pool.

### Regulation M-C (Sep 9 – Dec 2, 2026 — current)

- Added 29 Pokémon + 6 new Mega Evolutions (Mega Absol Z, Mega Salamence, Mega Garchomp Z, Mega Lucario Z, Mega Golisopod, Mega Baxcalibur).
- Current active regulation.

### Open Team Lists

- In TPCi events, players swap full team lists before each match (species, abilities, held items, all known moves, Stat Alignment).

## 6. Ladder / Ranking System

### Tier Structure (6 tiers)

| Tier        | Sub-ranks     | Notes                                                            |
| ----------- | ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Beginner    | None          | Tutorial only. No rewards.                                       |
| Poké Ball   | 4 → 3 → 2 → 1 | Real ladder starts here.                                         |
| Great Ball  | 4 → 3 → 2 → 1 | First box storage unlock (+5) at initial promotion.              |
| Ultra Ball  | 4 → 3 → 2 → 1 | Second box storage unlock (+5).                                  |
| Master Ball | 4 → 3 → 2 → 1 | Top ranks locked for first week. +10 storage at first promotion. |
| Champion    | None          | Highest tier. Locked first week. No sub-ranks.                   |

- **Singles and Doubles are ranked separately** — independent ladders, independent season rewards.

### Progression

- Win = +1/4 of rank gauge. Loss = -1/4.
- **Win Streak Bonus:** 2+ consecutive wins → each win fills gauge by 2/4 (halves the grind).
- Minimum net 48 wins (zero losses) to reach Champion.
- Demotion possible: lose enough and you drop a tier.

## 7. In-Game Currencies Affecting Box Management

| Currency                | Earned By                                                                | Spent On                                                                                           |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Victory Points (VP)** | Ranked wins/losses, missions, battle pass, first-time promotions, events | Permanent recruitment (2,500 VP), training (moves/stats/abilities), item shop, refresh ranch timer |
| **Quick Coupons**       | Tutorials, battle pass, competitions                                     | Skip 1 hour of ranch refresh timer (22 needed for full refresh)                                    |
| **Teammate Ticket**     | Tutorials, battle pass                                                   | Permanent recruitment (1 ticket = 1 Pokémon, bypasses VP cost)                                     |
| **Training Ticket**     | Battle pass, membership missions                                         | Training modifications (bypasses VP cost)                                                          |
| **Affinity Tickets**    | Achievements                                                             | Bias ranch lineup toward a type or Poké Ball                                                       |
| **Starter Pack**        | One-time purchase ($)                                                    | +50 box slots (permanent) + bundled perks                                                          |
| **Membership**          | Monthly/Annual ($4.99/$49.99)                                            | +1,000 box slots (temporary), +15 team slots, bonus missions                                       |

### Key VP Economics for Box Management

- **Recruit:** 2,500 VP per permanent Pokémon. Teammate Ticket bypasses VP.
- **Training:** Variable VP cost per change (moves, stats, abilities, items).
- **Returning:** Free — but permanent deletion; no VP refund.
- **Replica Team restoration:** 0 VP if current Pokémon match the saved config; costs VP for any mismatches.

## 8. Design Implications for Pokémon Box Manager

1. **Box limit is 30 (F2P) expandable to ~50 free via rank-ups, +50 via Starter Pack, +1000 via membership.** Hard cap at ~50 for F2P grinding. The app should track each user's current capacity and warn before hitting the limit.

2. **Returning to Ranch is permanent deletion.** The app should treat "Return to Ranch" as a destructive action (require confirmation, offer alternative of sending to HOME if applicable).

3. **HOME integration is critical.** Pokémon from HOME are the only truly safe overflow storage. The app should prioritize HOME-sourced Pokémon for "safe delete" suggestions.

4. **Replica Team IDs are the only F2P archive for team configs.** The app should help users save, tag, and organize Team IDs separately from the box.

5. **2,500 VP per permanent recruit** means users should be strategic about which Pokémon they recruit vs. trial. App should track VP budget.

6. **Species + Item clauses** mean the box manager's team builder must enforce: no dup species, no dup items.

7. **Separate Singles/Doubles ladders** means users may maintain separate rosters for each. App should support tagging Pokémon by format.

8. **Regulations rotate** (M-A → M-B → M-C, ~3 months each). App should track which Pokémon are legal in the current regulation and flag out-of-format box members.

9. **Trial Pokémon (7-day rental)** occupy box space and cannot be trained. App should track trial expiry dates and warn when trial Pokémon are about to expire.

10. **Ranked season resets monthly.** Box storage from rank-ups is likely permanent, but the app should confirm this with the user rather than assume.
